TPK's optional on-device video trimmer uses ffmpeg.wasm.

JavaScript wrapper: @ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15, MIT.
Project, source and license: https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm

WebAssembly processor: @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10, GPL-2.0-or-later.
Corresponding source and reproducible build scripts:
https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/tree/main/packages/core
https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/tree/main
FFmpeg source: https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/FFmpeg
License: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html

The pinned public npm distributions are installed at build time and their
unmodified processor files are served from /vendor/video-trimmer/. Media is
processed on the user's device, not sent to ffmpeg.wasm or a third-party CDN.
